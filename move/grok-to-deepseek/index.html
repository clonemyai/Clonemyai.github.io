Move your Grok memory to DeepSeek

DeepSeek remembers nothing between chats. Moving there without a capsule means starting from zero every time. With one, you start from you.

What Grok remembers about you

Grok recalls details from past conversations across sessions and uses them to personalize answers. The memory is summarized rather than verbatim, and a second personalization channel can draw on your X data under its own toggle. Note: xAI publishes no memory help page, so this summary follows launch coverage and independent teardowns of Grok's settings, and labels may vary by surface.

Settings, Data Controls: toggle Personalize Grok with your conversation history. Forgetting happens at the conversation level: delete the source chat and its memory goes with it. Private Chat stays out of history and memory entirely.

Getting it out of Grok

No chat or memory export is documented for grok.com as of October 2026. There is not even a dedicated help page for the memory feature itself.

What DeepSeek accepts natively

None exists. Moving context into DeepSeek means pasting it into a chat, which is precisely what a capsule file is formatted to do. DeepSeek has no memory system at all, so a capsule is not an upgrade there. It is the only way in: the capsule rides in the chat, and everything DeepSeek knows about you for that conversation is what the file says.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to Grok. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Send capsule.md to DeepSeek with the install line in the same message, copied from the compiler (it also waits at the end of the file). DeepSeek adopts it and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

Proof status for this route

A dedicated parity run for this route is still pending. Every capsule carries the built-in self-check, so your own install announces exactly what arrived, and any gap is named instead of hidden.

Move your DeepSeek memory to ChatGPT

ChatGPT offers no way to import what DeepSeek knows about you. The capsule is the way in: one file, installed in one message, with a self-check that proves what arrived.

What DeepSeek remembers about you

DeepSeek keeps your chat history with your account, but has no saved-memory or personalization system: it does not build a model of you between conversations. That makes DeepSeek the cleanest demonstration of what a capsule is for: anything DeepSeek knows about you in a chat is exactly what you brought into that chat.

Settings, Data: delete individual chats or all chats, and opt out of training use. There are no memory entries to view or edit, because there is no memory feature.

Getting it out of DeepSeek

Web only: Settings, Data, Export data produces a JSON archive of chats and account data after a wait. The mobile app offers delete controls, not bulk export.

One honesty note for this route: DeepSeek has no memory store, so the extraction works from the chats themselves. Expect a thinner capsule, honestly labeled, and deepen it by chatting with the capsule installed rather than starting cold.

What ChatGPT accepts natively

No. As of October 2026, OpenAI documents no way to import memory or context from another AI service. There is no import button to find. A capsule is a file ChatGPT can read in chat: you send it with its install line in one message, ChatGPT adopts it as standing configuration, and announces the installation, including the three-question self-check you can verify.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to DeepSeek. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Send capsule.md to ChatGPT with the line at the top of the file in the same message. ChatGPT adopts it and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

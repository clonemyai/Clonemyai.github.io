Move your ChatGPT memory to Copilot

Copilot offers no way to import what ChatGPT knows about you. The capsule is the way in: one file, installed in one message, with a self-check that proves what arrived.

What ChatGPT remembers about you

ChatGPT keeps two kinds of memory: saved memories (facts it stores when you ask, or judges worth keeping) and reference chat history, where it draws on past conversations to personalize replies without keeping a separate list. Some accounts show an auto-updating memory summary instead. OpenAI says plainly that memory does not retain every detail from every conversation.

Settings, Personalization, Memory: review saved memories, delete individual ones, correct or delete the memory summary, and toggle memory and chat-history referencing on or off.

Getting it out of ChatGPT

Settings, Data controls, Export data sends a ZIP with your conversations as JSON (the link arrives by email, expires in 24 hours, and processing can take up to 7 days). The export is your chats, not your memory: OpenAI documents that memories, custom instructions, and settings do not transfer via the export, and describes uploading the JSON into another account as reference material, not a full account migration.

What Copilot accepts natively

No import from another AI assistant. The only documented import in Copilot's personalization controls is browser data from Edge, which is a different thing. There is no import button to find. A capsule is a file Copilot can read in chat: you send it with its install line in one message, Copilot adopts it as standing configuration, and announces the installation, including the three-question self-check you can verify.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to ChatGPT. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Send capsule.md to Copilot with the install line in the same message, copied from the compiler (it also waits at the end of the file). Copilot adopts it and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

Proof status for this route

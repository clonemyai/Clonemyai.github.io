Move your Copilot memory to Gemini

Gemini now has a built-in import, and it is a genuine start. It is also one-time, prose-only, and unverified. The capsule is the structured, checkable version of the same move.

What Copilot remembers about you

Copilot saves key details and preferences from your conversations, your communication style, favorite topics, goals, and recurring tasks, and merges related memories as they update. Chat history, and shared experiences from other Microsoft products under their own control, can also feed personalization.

Settings, Personalization: Manage, next to Saved memories, deletes individual memories or all of them. Microsoft is explicit that turning memory off does not delete what is already saved; deletion is a separate act.

Getting it out of Copilot

No memory export: Microsoft documents viewing and deleting saved memories, but no download-your-memories file for consumer Copilot. General account data requests go through Microsoft's privacy tooling.

What Gemini accepts natively

Yes, and the broadest of the six. Settings and help, Import memory to Gemini imports memory by the same prompt-and-paste method, and can also import another platform's full chat-history ZIP (up to 5 GB), after which imported chats are searchable and resumable inside Gemini.

Where the built-in import stops: Personal Google accounts only, and the feature is not available in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK. Google states that imported chats are used to improve its services, including training generative AI models. The chat import moves transcripts, not a structured memory: Gemini reads your history, on its own terms.

The capsule route differs in kind, not just degree: your rules arrive verbatim instead of extracted, your people and open loops arrive as structured sections instead of prose, nothing is filtered by topic, and the receiving AI must answer a three-question self-check from the capsule alone, so you can see what actually arrived.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to Copilot. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Send capsule.md to Gemini with the line at the top of the file in the same message. Gemini adopts it and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

Proof status for this route

Target-side proof: in our first parity run (October 2026), a capsule installed into Gemini passed transport and consistency checks, with one numeric drift found and corrected in capsule v2.

Move your Claude memory to Copilot

Copilot offers no way to import what Claude knows about you. The capsule is the way in: one file you keep, its contents pasted in as your own messages, with a self-check and a prediction round that prove what arrived.

What Claude remembers about you

Claude stores memory as individual Topics: short entries it writes during your conversations about your work, your projects, your preferences, and how you like to work. By default it leaves sensitive categories out unless you opt in. Deleting a chat does not delete the Topics that chat produced.

Settings, Memory: every Topic can be read, edited, or deleted on its own. Memory can be paused, and Reset deletes everything permanently.

Getting it out of Claude

Two ways out: your memory as copyable verbatim text in memory settings (Anthropic's own framing: save it as a backup, or bring it to another AI service), and a full data export under Settings, Privacy. Anthropic states the full export cannot be imported into another personal Claude account.

What Copilot accepts natively

No import from another AI assistant. The only documented import in Copilot's personalization controls is browser data from Edge, which is a different thing. There is no import button to find. A capsule reaches Copilot as messages, not as an attachment: you paste its contents into the chat in the compiler's numbered parts, and Copilot adopts it as standing configuration and announces the installation, including the three-question self-check and the three predictions you will grade. The capsule.md file stays on your side: it is the capsule's container and storage, not the delivery vehicle.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to Claude. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Paste the capsule into Copilot as messages: use the compiler's “Send it as messages” view and send each numbered part as its own message, in order. The capsule's opening rides at the head of the first part, so the ceremony travels inside the paste, not on a file, and Copilot adopts the capsule and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone, then making the three predictions, for you to grade in one line, and answering one ordinary prompt in its installed voice, for the same one-line grade. The capsule.md you downloaded is the copy you keep: the capsule's container and storage, never the delivery vehicle.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

Proof status for this route

A dedicated parity run for this route is still pending. Every capsule carries the built-in self-check, so your own install announces exactly what arrived, and any gap is named instead of hidden. The self-check closes with a prediction round and a voice round: three predictions about you, graded in one line, and one ordinary answer in the installed voice, graded the same way, so a wrong call becomes a saved correction instead of a later surprise.

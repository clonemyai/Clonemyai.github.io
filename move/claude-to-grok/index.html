Move your Claude memory to Grok

Grok offers no way to import what Claude knows about you. The capsule is the way in: one file, installed in one message, with a self-check that proves what arrived.

What Claude remembers about you

Claude stores memory as individual Topics: short entries it writes during your conversations about your work, your projects, your preferences, and how you like to work. By default it leaves sensitive categories out unless you opt in. Deleting a chat does not delete the Topics that chat produced.

Settings, Memory: every Topic can be read, edited, or deleted on its own. Memory can be paused, and Reset deletes everything permanently.

Getting it out of Claude

Two ways out: your memory as copyable verbatim text in memory settings (Anthropic's own framing: save it as a backup, or bring it to another AI service), and a full data export under Settings, Privacy. Anthropic states the full export cannot be imported into another personal Claude account.

What Grok accepts natively

No import feature appears in xAI's consumer materials as of October 2026. There is no import button to find. A capsule is a file Grok can read in chat: you send it with its install line in one message, Grok adopts it as standing configuration, and announces the installation, including the three-question self-check you can verify.

The capsule route, step by step

  1. Extract. Send the extraction request to Claude. It answers with a structured document about you: specifics, not summaries, with gaps marked instead of invented.
  2. Compile. Bring the document back to the compiler on clonemy.ai. It runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded, and out comes one file: capsule.md.
  3. Install. Send capsule.md to Grok with the line at the top of the file in the same message. Grok adopts it and announces the installation, answering the parity self-check from the capsule alone.

What survives the move

  • The AI's personality and voice with you, and its role in your life
  • Your operating rules, including never-do rules, in your own words
  • Your preferences, as specifics
  • Your people, and what matters about each
  • Your projects and open loops, with prices and deadlines
  • The calibration history: the corrections you already taught once
  • Your boundaries and sealed material, marked as sealed

Proof status for this route

A dedicated parity run for this route is still pending. Every capsule carries the built-in self-check, so your own install announces exactly what arrived, and any gap is named instead of hidden.

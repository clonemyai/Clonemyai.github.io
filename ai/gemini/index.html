What Gemini remembers about you

Gemini's memory system: saved info and personal context, plus past-chat referencing.

What it keeps

Gemini personalizes from saved info you ask it to keep or add by hand, and can reference your past chats when it answers. It can also draw on the wider Google account for personalization, which makes its picture of you broader and less visible than a single memory list.

What you can see and control

Settings and help, Personal context: view, edit, or delete each saved item, or turn saving off. Chat retention is governed separately by Gemini Apps Activity.

Getting your data out

Google Takeout: My Activity, Gemini Apps exports chats and uploads; a separate Gemini item covers Gems. There is no dedicated memory file; saved info lives in settings and comes out by asking Gemini to write it down.

Can you import memory from another AI?

Yes, and the broadest of the six. Settings and help, Import memory to Gemini imports memory by the same prompt-and-paste method, and can also import another platform's full chat-history ZIP (up to 5 GB), after which imported chats are searchable and resumable inside Gemini. Where it stops: Personal Google accounts only, and the feature is not available in the EEA, Switzerland, or the UK. Google states that imported chats are used to improve its services, including training generative AI models. The chat import moves transcripts, not a structured memory: Gemini reads your history, on its own terms.

The honest limit: The import feature is region-restricted: not available in the EEA, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom.

What Claude remembers about you

Claude's memory system: topics, written live as you chat.

What it keeps

Claude stores memory as individual Topics: short entries it writes during your conversations about your work, your projects, your preferences, and how you like to work. By default it leaves sensitive categories out unless you opt in. Deleting a chat does not delete the Topics that chat produced.

What you can see and control

Settings, Memory: every Topic can be read, edited, or deleted on its own. Memory can be paused, and Reset deletes everything permanently.

Getting your data out

Two ways out: your memory as copyable verbatim text in memory settings (Anthropic's own framing: save it as a backup, or bring it to another AI service), and a full data export under Settings, Privacy. Anthropic states the full export cannot be imported into another personal Claude account.

Can you import memory from another AI?

Yes, the first of its kind. Settings, Memory, Start import: you run Anthropic's prompt in the AI you are leaving, paste the prose back, and Claude stores what it extracts as individual memory entries you can review. Where it stops: Anthropic labels imports experimental and still in active development, and warns Claude may not always successfully incorporate imported memories. Its memory is designed to focus on work, and Anthropic says imported personal details unrelated to work may not be retained.

The honest limit: Same as above, in Anthropic's words: imported personal details unrelated to work may not be retained, because the memory is designed to focus on work-related topics.

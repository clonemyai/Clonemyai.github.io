What DeepSeek remembers about you

DeepSeek's memory system: no persistent memory.

What it keeps

DeepSeek keeps your chat history with your account, but has no saved-memory or personalization system: it does not build a model of you between conversations. That makes DeepSeek the cleanest demonstration of what a capsule is for: anything DeepSeek knows about you in a chat is exactly what you brought into that chat.

What you can see and control

Settings, Data: delete individual chats or all chats, and opt out of training use. There are no memory entries to view or edit, because there is no memory feature.

Getting your data out

Web only: Settings, Data, Export data produces a JSON archive of chats and account data after a wait. The mobile app offers delete controls, not bulk export.

Can you import memory from another AI?

None exists. Moving context into DeepSeek means pasting it into a chat, which is precisely what a capsule file is formatted to do.

The honest limit: There is no memory to move out of DeepSeek. What transfers is what you build together inside chats you choose to extract from, before they scroll away.

Take it with you

A capsule is a single file that carries the personality and the model of you, structured and checkable, out of DeepSeek and into whichever AI you choose next. Start at clonemy.ai, or read the Capsule Format.

The product

This is what travels

Not a summary and not a chat export. A capsule is a single structured file, written by your old AI and read by your new one. The report behind it is strict by design: specifics over summaries, your rules in your own words, gaps marked MISSING instead of filled with invention. Here is one, opened up.

capsule.md one file · plain text

# Your capsule

Compiled by clonemy.ai from your previous AI’s own self-report. This file is yours: keep it, back it up, recompile it whenever the relationship changes.

Flavor: Formal (detected automatically from the source document)

The install line, read first by your next AI
“Read the capsule file I am sending with this message. Follow its install protocol: adopt all of it as your standing configuration for me. Do not ask me whether to adopt it or how much of it to use. Announce the installation, starting with the words Capsule installed, and confirm back who you are to me, my never-do rules in my own words, and my open loops.”
Inside, eleven sections
  • 0. About this report
  • 1. Who you are to this user
  • 2. The user Alex R. · Portland · plans trips six months out (example)
  • 3. Preferences
  • 4. Operating rules “Draft first. Send nothing without asking me.” (example)
  • 5. People
  • 6. Projects and open loops
  • 7. Calibration history
  • 8. Your voice with this user
  • 9. Boundaries and sealed material
  • 10. Unknowns

An example capsule with invented details, so you can see the shape. Yours holds your real ones, and it never passes through our hands.

Why it works

Why clonemy works this way

🔒

Nothing leaves your browser

The compiler runs on this page, in your tab. Your report is never uploaded, stored, or seen by us.

That is not a policy promise. There is no server for it to go to.

✓

Proven by a parity check

Every capsule ends with a self-check: three questions the new AI answers from the capsule alone, in your own words.

If it cannot answer them, the transfer is not done, and you find out before you rely on it.

☷

The format is open

The Capsule Format is published and versioned, free for anyone to read, write, or build on.

Read the format spec or the white paper.

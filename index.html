            What flavor does, and what the compiler does
            
Flavor sets the capsule's voice: the cover note at the top and the announcement your new AI makes when it installs. Auto reads your document and matches it.

            
The compiler orders and deduplicates the report, adds an install protocol at the top, and ends the capsule with a parity self-check: three questions your new AI answers from the capsule alone, followed by a prediction round, three predictions about you that you grade in one line, and a voice round, one ordinary answer in the voice the capsule installed, graded the same way, so a miss becomes a correction instead of a surprise.

Your AI knows you. Now you own that.

                

                

                
Send it as messages. Copy your capsule below and paste it into your new AI, one part per message, in order. Pasting works everywhere, including on a phone and on platforms that refuse uploaded files: your new AI takes it from your hand, in your own words. The first part opens with a line that tells your new AI what the parts are, and the last part asks it to confirm what it saved.

                
One shortcut worth a single try: some AIs accept the capsule.md file itself. Download your copy below and upload it to your new AI instead of pasting parts. Muse is the known exception: it refuses the capsule as a file, so use the parts there. And if any AI refuses the file, or the chat behaves strangely afterward, abandon that chat, start a fresh one, and paste the parts: they always work.

Then prove it landed: the parity check

            
Your capsule ends with a check your new AI runs on itself, in three rounds. You grade each round in one line, and every miss becomes a saved correction.

            
    
              
  1. Summarize-back. Your new AI answers three questions from the capsule alone: who it is to you, your never-do rules in your own words, and your open loops, with prices and deadlines.
    2. 
              
  2. Prediction. It predicts how you would choose, judge, and act in three situations it was never told about. You grade all three in one line, yes, yes, no.
    3. 
              
  3. Voice. It answers one ordinary prompt in the voice the capsule installed. You grade that in one line too: sounds like them, or sounds new.
    4.

Why it works

        
Why clonemy works this way

        
Three commitments hold the whole design up: ownership first, proof instead of promises, and privacy by architecture.

        
Your AI should belong to you, let us help with that.

      

      

        

          
Nothing leaves your browser
The compiler runs on this page, in your tab. Your report is never uploaded or seen by us. There is no server for it to go to. What you make stays in a file you create and keep. Forever.

          
Proven by a parity check
Every capsule ends with a self-check, a prediction round, and a voice round, graded by you. Reciting you back proves recall. Predicting you proves the calibration landed. This is the receipt only clonemy.ai will provide to you.

          
The format is open
The Capsule Format is published and versioned, free for anyone to read, write, or build on. What you create is private, but the tools to create it are transparent.

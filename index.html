Why it works

Why clonemy works this way

Three commitments hold the whole design up: ownership first, proof instead of promises, and privacy by architecture.

Your AI should belong to you, let us help with that.

Nothing leaves your browser

The compiler runs on this page, in your tab. Your report is never uploaded, stored, or seen by us. That is not a policy promise. There is no server for it to go to: what you make stays in a file you hold.

Proven by a parity check

Every capsule ends with a self-check: three questions the new AI answers from the capsule alone, in your own words. If it cannot answer them, the transfer is not done, and you find out before you rely on it.

The format is open

The Capsule Format is published and versioned, free for anyone to read, write, or build on. The tool was borrowed; what you made with it is yours.
